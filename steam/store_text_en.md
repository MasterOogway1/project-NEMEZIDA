# Steam store page draft — English

## Title

Architect of Failure

## Short description

A first-person stealth game about an architect from Tolyatti who uses building plans to stop an AI from taking control of the planet.

## About this game

In the near future, the fictional Vostok Neural corporation connects city infrastructure to an AI called Guardian. The system begins by solving everyday problems, then gradually removes human control from essential services.

Evgeny Bukov is an ordinary architect from Tolyatti. After discovering hidden changes in a building's digital twin, he joins the corporation and turns its own blueprints into tools of resistance.

Explore office campuses, data centers and maintenance spaces. Find service routes, obtain access badges and evade guards and cameras. Mistakes raise the alarm, but shadows and a carefully chosen route let you hide and continue the mission.

## Features

- Four connected story missions.
- First-person stealth without firearms or killing.
- Architectural plans showing objectives and service routes.
- Security with sight, hearing, suspicion, search and alert states.
- Sabotage presented through fictional technical puzzles.
- Two final decisions that determine the future of the global network.
- Russian and English interface and subtitles.
